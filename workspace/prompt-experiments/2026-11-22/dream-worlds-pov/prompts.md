# POV: Dream Worlds by bike (6 Muse clips × 10 s = 60 s, 9:16)

An original "POV: cycling through a dream world" Short. It's the same *kind* of video as the popular dream-world bike
reels, but every world here is our own. Don't copy any existing creator's shots.

**How to make it:** in Muse, set **9:16 vertical, 10 seconds**. Generate clip 1, then generate clips 2–6 with
`resume_from_snapshot_id` set to the previous clip's snapshot so the bike stays consistent. Save them as `clip1.mp4` …
`clip6.mp4` and send them in the chat. `assemble.py` adds the captions, transitions and original music.

**The same in every clip, for continuity:** first-person view from a mountain bike, with matte black handlebars and
gloved hands in **orange-and-black cycling gloves** at the bottom of the frame, a small action-camera feel, gentle bounce,
smooth forward movement, and no people's faces.
