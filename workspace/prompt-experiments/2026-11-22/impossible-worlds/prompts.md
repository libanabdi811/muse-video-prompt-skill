# Impossible Worlds: surreal "bent world" Short (6 scenes, 9:16, ~60 s)

The style is photoreal, surreal "impossible landscapes": the world bends or folds like the inside of a giant tube, places
that can't exist, a tiny person for scale, and very slow, smooth camera movement. It's the same style as the popular
dreamwave reels, but every scene below is our own idea (don't reuse any creator's frames).

**Workflow (Higgsfield):** generate each image (9:16, photoreal) → animate it with image-to-video (10 s, slow camera move,
no audio) → `assemble.py` adds the music, crossfades and captions.

**Cost:** 6 images × 0.75 = 4.5 credits, plus 6 clips × 10 = 60 credits, ≈ 65 credits in total (≈ 80 with retries).

## 1. The Rolled-Up Island
**Image:** Photoreal aerial view of a tropical island coastline that bends upward and curls overhead like the inside of a giant cylinder; turquoise lagoons, white sand beaches and dense green jungle continue up the curved wall and across the sky; soft natural daylight, light haze, a tiny person in a white shirt standing on the beach in the foreground for scale; ultra-detailed, cinematic, 35mm film look, no text.
**Motion:** Very slow forward drone push along the beach toward the curve, gentle waves rolling on the sand, the clouds drifting; smooth and stable.

## 2. The Moon Pool
**Image:** Photoreal wide shot of a turquoise infinity swimming pool built on the grey dusty surface of the Moon, its edge overhanging a deep crater; a pastel pink-and-white pool house and sun loungers beside it; the Earth huge and bright in the black starry sky; a tiny swimmer floating in the pool; soft clean light, surreal and calm, ultra-detailed, no text.
**Motion:** Slow lateral dolly along the pool edge, the water rippling gently, a few moon-dust particles drifting, Earth slowly rising.

## 3. The Sunken Meadow
**Image:** Photoreal underwater scene: a bright green meadow with wildflowers and a single old tree on the ocean floor, sunbeams streaming down through the clear turquoise water, a school of silver fish circling the tree, a tiny scuba diver hovering beside it for scale; dreamy, peaceful, ultra-detailed, no text.
**Motion:** Slow forward glide toward the tree, light rays shimmering, the grass and leaves swaying in the current, fish swirling.

## 4. Stairs to Saturn
**Image:** Photoreal surreal landscape: a white marble spiral staircase rising from golden desert dunes high into a clear deep-blue sky that fades into outer space, Saturn huge with glowing rings at the top; a tiny person in a red coat climbing halfway up; golden-hour light, long shadows, ultra-detailed, no text.
**Motion:** Slow upward tilt and crane following the staircase into space, sand drifting off the dunes, the stars slowly appearing.

## 5. The Ocean Wall
**Image:** Photoreal scene of the ocean folding upward into a vertical wall of water that rises into the clouds, a huge glassy wave frozen in time, sunlight glowing through the turquoise water; a tiny surfer at its base and a long white sand beach in the foreground; epic scale, ultra-detailed, no text.
**Motion:** Very slow push toward the water wall, the surface shimmering and moving upward, spray drifting, the surfer gliding.

## 6. The Lake in the Sky
**Image:** Photoreal green alpine valley at sunrise where a whole mountain lake hangs upside down in the sky above, its water surface facing the ground, small waterfalls pouring down from it into the valley, clouds around it; a tiny hiker on a grassy hill looking up; soft golden light, ultra-detailed, no text.
**Motion:** Slow crane up from the hiker toward the floating lake, the waterfalls falling, mist rising, gentle wind in the grass.
