# Drawbridge Getaway — animated short film (15 s)

**Idea:** a getaway driver has to beat a police chase across a drawbridge before the deck lifts at sunset.

The film has a GTA-style open-world crime mood, but everything in it is original: the city, the characters and the car. It has no Rockstar characters, logos, cars or place names, so it is safe to post on Leonida Unlocked as an original fan-mood short.

Format is 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds, 24 fps, with ambient sound only and no dialogue.

---

## 1. Story (5 beats)

1. **Hook.** Marisol "Mari" Reyes looks into the rearview mirror. Red and blue lights fill it.
2. **Run.** Her coral muscle coupe drifts onto the Port Coral causeway. Palm trees streak past, and a helicopter spotlight hunts her.
3. **Problem.** Bridge bells ring. The gate arm drops, and the bridge deck starts to rise in front of her.
4. **Leap.** She floors it. The car launches off the rising deck, hanging in slow motion over the water with the sunset behind it.
5. **Payoff.** The car lands hard on the far side in a burst of sparks. The police cars skid to a stop at the gap. Mari exhales and smirks.

## 4. Master video prompt (one 15-second clip)

Attach the three reference images: `@Mari` for the character sheet, `@Coupe` for the car sheet and `@Bridge` for the location sheet. Seedance 2.0 in Dreamina or CapCut takes reference images and 15-second clips. For Google Flow (Veo), cut this prompt into two 8-second halves, 0–8 s and 7–15 s.

```
[STYLE LOCK] 9:16 vertical, 15 seconds, 24fps, one continuous action
sequence. Main character @Mari, car @Coupe, location @Bridge. Keep the
character, car and bridge identical to the references in every shot.

[0–3s] Extreme close-up of a rearview mirror: Mari's focused eyes, red and
blue police lights flooding the mirror, sweat on her brow. She grips the
wheel; a quick push-in on her eyes.

[3–6s] Low tracking shot beside the coral coupe as it drifts onto the
palm-lined causeway, tire smoke, palms streaking past, a helicopter
spotlight sweeping across the road, three police cruisers behind.

[6–9s] Wide shot from ahead: warning bells flash, the striped gate arm
drops, the drawbridge deck begins to rise. The coupe smashes through the
gate arm, splinters flying, and accelerates up the tilting deck.

[9–12s] Side-on slow motion: the coupe launches off the edge of the rising
deck and hangs in the air over the turquoise bay, the giant sunset sun
directly behind it, silhouette with glowing rim light, pelicans
scattering.

[12–15s] Real speed: the car slams down on the far side in a shower of
sparks and skids to a stop. Police cruisers brake hard at the gap. Close
on Mari through the windshield: she exhales, then a small relieved smirk.
Hold on her face as the sunset flares.

Sound: rising engine roar, sirens, bridge bells, wind rush during the
jump, heavy landing impact, then sudden quiet with gulls.
Camera: dynamic, smooth, cinematic; no cuts to unrelated scenes.
Avoid: text, logos, real car brands, extra fingers, warped faces,
changing outfit or car colour, cartoonish exaggeration.
```
